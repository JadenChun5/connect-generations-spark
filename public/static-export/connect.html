Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

Simply fill out the contact form above, and our team will reach out to schedule a demo and walk you through the setup process. We'll have you up and running in no time!

Is there a cost to use ElderConnect?

We offer flexible pricing plans for schools and senior centers. Contact us to learn more about options that fit your organization's needs and budget.

How is volunteer hour tracking verified?

Senior center staff confirm each visit through the platform, creating an official record that can be exported for college applications, awards, and other programs.

What safety measures are in place?

We prioritize safety with verified accounts, parent notifications for minors, secure communications, and coordination with senior center staff. All volunteers must follow center-specific requirements and protocols.
